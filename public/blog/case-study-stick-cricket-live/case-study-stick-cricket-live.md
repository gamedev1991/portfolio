# Introduction

Downloaded over a million times on Google Play, with a rating of 4.3, and been awarded the Best Competitive Game In India for 2019, this is one cricket game any cricket lover should not miss.

Stick Cricket Live is a real-time 1v1 multiplayer cricket game, where each player gets 3 overs to bat, the player with the highest score at the end of the third over wins. If a game is tied it's decided by a superover. The controls are simple with just two buttons, left button, and the right button. Tapping the button in the direction of the delivery at the right time leads to the batsman playing a shot accordingly. There's a vertical bar on the left screen that shows your timing. The better the timing better the shot the batsman plays, imagine a reverse-sweep six.

A player can enter a game by betting a certain amount of coins depending on the stadium the player chooses, the stadiums get unlocked with a player's trophies that are earned by winning a game. Trophies are lost when a game is lost. A player gets the double amount of coins betted and a cricket kit that has different cards, these cards are different bowlers or bats that a player can choose to have when playing a game. A player can choose the bowler according to the bat of the opponent and gets 10 seconds at the start of every over. Some bowlers are strong against a particular bat while some are weak and hence it's important to keep collecting cards to upgrade the bowlers as well as the bat. While collecting cards is important, upgrading them requires coins that a player gets after winning matches, hence the loop- play to earn coins, kits, and trophies, upgrade cards using coins and then play again to unlock the next stadium.

The game uses the treasure chest retention strategy of Clash Royale where a player's kit takes 4 hours to open, a pro kit takes 8, and an elite extra kit bag takes 24 hours to open.

Along with this, another retention strategy implemented by the game is the free kits bag that a player gets every 4 hours. To increase engagement, the game also rewards players with a 'runs' kit bag that gets unlocked after a player scores 500 runs which is a great motivation to play a few more matches as the coins rewarded in this kit are higher than the other kits.

In addition to the above strategies, here are a few more strategies that can increase engagement and retention in the game:

## Strategy 1: Boosters

There are 2 simple boosters that can be implemented to increase engagement in the game:

- **Victory Coin Booster**: Reward 2x coins for a specific time interval (eg: 24 hours), so for 24 hours if a player wins, then the player gets 2x of the reward amount, so if a player was getting 400 coins on betting 200, now the player will get 600 (200+400) coins. These boosters can be available in the store and can be bought using x gems where x can be decided after multivariate testing. These boosters will make players play more during that 24 hour period as they will be rewarded double the amount of coins.

- **Kit Speed Up Booster**: Reduce the time to unlock kits by 1/2, so if a kit used to take 4 hours to unlock, after the booster it will take 2 hours only. This booster can be awarded to a player whenever the player unlocks a new stadium, thus motivating players to unlock more stadiums and increasing engagement. This booster can be made available in the store as well where it can be bought for x gems which can be decided after multivariate testing.

The KPI for the above boosters would be the number of times the boosters are bought.

## Strategy 2: Not Enough Coins

The not enough coins pop up asks users to go to the store, instead of that a better UX would be showing a button to buy the remaining coins using the appropriate amount of gems in the same pop up itself. This is for users that are just a few coins short of the bet amount and will have to either purchase coins from the store or play a game with a lower bet amount, giving the users a third option where users can buy the coins when they are short will allow them to bet slightly higher and increase engagement. This strategy has been implemented very well in Tennis Clash. The key metrics to monitor would be:

- Number of drop-offs when a user has a fewer amount of coins
- Number of users that opt for buying the deficit amount of coins

## Strategy 3: Subscription (Pass)

Subscription is a revenue model that has become very popular among users. Subscriptions allow users to access or have a premium version of a game in exchange for a monthly fee.

PUBG's revenue got boosted by 365% after the introduction of Royale Pass and hence is a very effective model to increase monetization.

The game can implement a season pass, where the users get additional cards and coins on their rewards, the runs kit can actually be changed to a series of rewards based on the number of runs scored in a season. The series of rewards can vary depending upon whether a user has subscribed for the pass or not. Both Clash Royale and Tennis Clash have executed the subscription model very well. Stick Cricket can use the same model and increase monetization in their game.

## Conclusion

Overall, Stick Cricket Live is a fun game to play, implementing the above strategies could enhance the game's engagement and also increase the monetization for the game.
